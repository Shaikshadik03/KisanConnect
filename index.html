KisanConnect 🌾

Fair Prices for Farmers, Fresh Produce for You

A Direct Farm-to-Consumer Marketplace with Real-Time Middleman Price Transparency.

Team: Antigravity Innovators • Track: Agri-Tech / Supply Chain
The Challenge

Intermediaries Squeeze Both Farmers & Consumers

  • 🔴
    Farmer Underpayment: Indian farmers receive only 20–30% of the retail price paid by end consumers.
  • 🔴
    3–4 Layers of Intermediaries: Village aggregators, commission agents, mandi wholesalers, and retailers take cumulative 50–70% margins.
  • 🔴
    Lose-Lose Supply Chain: Consumers pay inflated food prices while farmers remain trapped in agrarian debt.
Market Gap

Why Existing Agri-Portals Fall Short

  • ⚠️
    Complex & English-Heavy: Most agri apps are difficult for rural farmers to navigate on basic mobile devices.
  • ⚠️
    No Price Transparency: Current portals fail to show the direct markup contrast between mandi rates and consumer retail.
  • ⚠️
    Lack of Localized Match: Consumers cannot discover and buy directly from nearby growers within their own district.
Proposed Solution

Transparent Direct Market & Price Comparison

  • ✅
    Live Price Comparison Badges: Transparently shows Farmer Price vs Mandi Wholesale vs Supermarket Retail for every crop.
  • ✅
    Direct Connection: Zero commission platform connects bulk buyers and households directly to the harvest source.
  • ✅
    Smart Proximity Discovery: Ranks produce by nearby distance and freshness date.
Technical Details

High Performance & Mobile-First Architecture

  • 💻
    Frontend: Clean semantic HTML5, Custom CSS, and ES6 JavaScript optimized for 3G/4G networks.
  • ⚙️
    Pricing Engine: Real-time savings and extra-margin calculation formulas executing client-side.
  • 📊
    Analytics: Interactive Chart.js graphs displaying farmer revenue expansion vs traditional mandi losses.
  • 📁
    Data Tier: Modular JSON datasets (`listings.json`, `market_prices.json`) and LocalStorage persistence.
Future Scope

Empowering India's Agricultural Backbone

  • 🚀
    Social Impact: Increases net farmer income by +40% while lowering household food inflation.
  • 🚀
    e-NAM & Agmarknet APIs: Dynamic synchronization with real-time national mandi benchmark prices.
  • 🚀
    Farm-Gate Logistics Aggregation: Partnering with rural EV freight logistics for consolidated doorstep delivery.
